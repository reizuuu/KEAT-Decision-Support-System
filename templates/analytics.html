Help & Support

Analytics Overview

Browse products by category to see what tends to sell alongside them, based on combos (patterns) found in your transaction data.

How to Browse

  • Categories: Click a category to see its products
  • Products: Click a product to see what it's frequently bought with
  • Search: Find a product directly by name

Reading a Row

A row like "+ Burger → Coke — 45%" means: when this product is bought together with Burger, Coke is bought 45% of the time.
